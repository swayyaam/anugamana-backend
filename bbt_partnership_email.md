**What I Have Built**

I have developed an application called **Anugamana** (अनुगमन — *to be guided along the path*), an AI-powered spiritual guidance platform built entirely on Srila Prabhupada's *Bhagavad Gita As It Is*.

The core experience is this: a person describes a life situation, an emotion, or a question in plain language — in English, Hindi, Tamil, Bengali, or any of 10 Indian languages — and the application finds the most relevant verses from the Gita along with a guidance note grounded faithfully in Srila Prabhupada's purports.

For example:

> *"I keep failing at work and feel like giving up"*
> → The application surfaces Bg 2.47, plays the Sanskrit verse aloud in correct pronunciation, shows Prabhupada's translation and purport, and offers guidance drawn directly from the commentary.

A few points I want to emphasise regarding how the application handles the sacred content:

- **The AI does not invent or add to Prabhupada's teachings.** Every guidance note is explicitly constrained to only use information from the retrieved verse and purport. If a verse does not address the question, the application says so honestly.
- **Full attribution is displayed** — verse reference, Srila Prabhupada's name, and the source (*Bhagavad Gita As It Is*) are shown on every result.
- **Sanskrit recitation is authentic** — I use a specialised Indian AI system (Sarvam AI) for text-to-speech that handles Sanskrit pronunciation correctly, unlike generic Western TTS systems.
- **The multilingual support is intentional** — the Gita's primary audience is Indian. A person in rural Maharashtra or Tamil Nadu who thinks in Marathi or Tamil deserves to access these teachings in their own language.

---

**The Broader Vision**

I am not limiting this to the Bhagavad Gita alone.

My intention is to expand Anugamana to become a comprehensive AI-powered study companion for **all of Srila Prabhupada's books** — the Srimad Bhagavatam, Sri Caitanya Caritamrta, the Nectar of Devotion, the Nectar of Instruction, and the full library of his writings.

The goal is a single platform where any seeker — regardless of their language or level of familiarity with the scriptures — can ask a genuine question and be guided to the exact passage where Prabhupada has already answered it.

This is, in a sense, a digital extension of what Prabhupada himself intended: that his books should reach every person, in every language, in every situation.

I am not seeking to commercialise the sacred texts. I am seeking permission to build the best possible technological means of connecting people to them.
